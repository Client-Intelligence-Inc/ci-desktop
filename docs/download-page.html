Installation

1

Download the DMG

Click the download button above. Your browser will download the installer.

2

Open and drag to Applications

Double-click the DMG file and drag Client Intelligence to your Applications folder.

3

Launch and sign in

Open Client Intelligence from Applications and sign in with your account. The app is signed and notarized by Apple — no security warnings.

4

Stay up to date

The app automatically checks for updates and installs them in the background. You'll get a notification when a new version is ready.
